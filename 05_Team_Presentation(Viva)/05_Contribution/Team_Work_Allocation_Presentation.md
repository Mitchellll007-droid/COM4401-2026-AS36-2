## 3. Preparation timeline (Week 4 → Week 5)

| When | What to have done |
|------|-------------------|
| **Week 4 — launch** | Agree who presents which part · choose the strongest evidence per project · draft slides · decide the live demo · book a rehearsal slot |
| **Between sessions** | Build slides · record the backup demo · draft the reflection & GAI statement |
| **Week 5 — delivery** | Finish & tidy the GitHub portfolio · rehearse with a timer · deliver the talk and demo · answer the Q&A as a team |

> The presentation is marked as one team piece against the oral-presentation grid (content & coverage, live demo & evidence, technical understanding & Q&A, reflection & GAI, structure & timing, slides, teamwork). Individual marks may then be adjusted for contribution.
